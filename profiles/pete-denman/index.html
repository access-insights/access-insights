About

Design as the framework, not the decoration.

Pete is a third generation Oregonian and a graduate of Portland State University in fine art and design, with a minor in computer science. He spent twenty years at Intel, joining its founding user centered design practice and moving into Intel Labs, where he led design and research on exploratory prototypes across health, accessibility, AI, and data visualization.

His best known work came from a request no designer forgets: working directly with Stephen Hawking's team to design the complete user experience for the custom software Professor Hawking used as his sole means of communication and computer control. Pete served as Intel's spokesperson for the project and was the first to propose releasing the system free and open source, which Intel did. He still supports the software today.

Disability is not a subject Pete studies from the outside. He is disabled and dyslexic, uses a power chair, and has spent his career turning that lived experience into a research instrument: leading intern teams through designing for a user, running in-the-wild studies, and advocating in Salem and Washington for people whose access depends on decisions made without them.

Selected work

Prototypes with people inside them.

  • Keep Hawking Talking

    Intel Labs

    Led the design of the interface Stephen Hawking used to communicate and control his computer. Featured in the documentary Hawking and the biography An Unfettered Mind; released free and open source at Pete's urging.

  • Connected Wheelchair

    Intel · team lead

    Research with power chair users and a five intern program that became Intel's Connected Wheelchair proof of concept, endorsed on stage by Stephen Hawking at IDF 2014. Read the case study.

  • WayFinding

    Intel Labs · senior lead designer

    Led UX and project management on technology helping people with low or no vision navigate interior spaces, connecting deep technical innovation to a usable experience.

  • Data Sense

    Intel Labs · lead designer

    A simple interaction controlling a complex data analysis tool, plus an invented biomimicry based visualization that renders large data sets in a natural, instantly readable form. Featured at IxDA Dublin.

  • Emotional Understanding

    Intel Labs · design lead

    Designed an in-the-wild study platform capturing natural human emotion for affective computing research, with in-situ prompts that raised participant compliance to nearly one hundred percent.

Leadership and advocacy

The work continues off the clock.

Boards and policy
  • QUAD Inc., chairman of the board (2015 to present): five apartment complexes housing nearly 200 people with disabilities and providing 100 jobs.
  • United Spinal Association, national board (2022 to present).
  • Disability Health & Employment Equity Coalition, co-founder (2022 to present): introduced Oregon SB 576, letting people with disabilities work while keeping essential benefits.
  • International Dyslexia Association, Oregon board (2019 to 2020).
  • UNESCO, technical advisor (Paris, 2015): brought design methods to two global communication projects.
