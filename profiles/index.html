The people behind Access Insights

Access Insights is a research and design agency built by the people who helped write the rules of accessibility. We came together after decades at Intel Corporation: researchers, designers, engineers, and disability rights pioneers, including the chief architect of the ADA. We have shaped the laws millions rely on and the tools billions use, and several of us bring lived experience of disability to every project we touch.
